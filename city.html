Edmonton at a Glance

Edmonton is a vibrant city that combines natural scenery with urban attractions, making it a great destination for short trips or weekend travel. The river valley offers extensive trails and viewpoints that feel surprisingly close to downtown. The city also has lively neighbourhoods with local restaurants, cafés, and shops that are easy to explore on foot.

What Visitors Enjoy

  • Exploring the River Valley trails and viewpoints
  • Visiting local restaurants, cafés, and shops
  • Checking out festivals and events around the city
  • Visiting museums and other cultural attractions

Best Time to Visit

Summer is a great time to visit Edmonton because the weather is warmer and there are many outdoor activities and festivals. Spring and fall can also be enjoyable for exploring the city with fewer crowds. Winter is colder, but visitors can still enjoy indoor attractions and winter events.

One-Day Itinerary

  1. Start the day with a walk through the River Valley
  2. Have lunch and explore the shops around Whyte Avenue
  3. Visit a museum or the Muttart Conservatory
  4. End the day with dinner at a local restaurant
